## Direction contract

The first viewport makes geography usable: a broad dark map, ingredient filters and a directly linked regional recipe drawer. The selected recipe exposes actual ingredient amounts and an explicitly ingredient-derived sketch. A comparison action opens two source-backed recipe versions, their profiles at a common scale and aligned ingredient rows. Selecting one ingredient traces it across the shapes and rows. The map remains the principal discovery object; the paired shapes and differences lead the comparison viewport. Warm ivory text, dark green surfaces, sand selection, sage map fills, thin quiet rules and the original Sifr type character carry across both routes. Mobile stacks map, selector and recipe details; comparison keeps controls and profiles legible with no horizontal page overflow.
