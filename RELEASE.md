# Sifr Atlas — Common Ground Makeathon 2026

Release candidate: **0.3.0**. Build, deployment and judge-access verification are recorded separately; this file does not establish a published release.

## What changed today

The pre-existing Sifr instrument contains two authored illustrative dish profiles and 24 preserved source records. Today's makeathon work adds a 100-recipe ingredient atlas, geographic discovery, source-linked recipe detail, and an ingredient comparison experience. The original instrument is preserved at `/instrument` and the original live Site is unchanged.

## Model boundaries

Recipe ingredient names and quantity strings come from the indexed sources. Geographic counts describe this recipe sample, not national popularity or cultural importance. Source area labels are navigation context and do not establish exclusive origin.

The ingredient-derived sketch assigns equal initial drawing contributions and fixed illustrative descriptor positions. It does not interpret quantities, cooking, serving conditions or perceptual interactions. A model-weight control changes the drawing, not a measured concentration or predicted taste. The original 24-record instrument is a separate authored model.
